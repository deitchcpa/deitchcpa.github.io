About the firm

Tax expertise you can rely on.

Deitch CPA delivers tax work that's proactive, personal, and clear. The firm brings deep tax experience — across national and regional practice — to work that stays close and responsive.

The practice specializes in small business and real estate taxation: entity structuring, pass-through planning, cost recovery, and the deal-level decisions that shape an owner's tax picture. We guarantee you won't be handed off to a rotating cast you never speak to — the people who plan your taxes are the people who prepare them.

Bob Deitch holds an active CPA license and a Master of Taxation, and is dedicated to serving individuals and closely-held businesses across the DC metro area and beyond.
